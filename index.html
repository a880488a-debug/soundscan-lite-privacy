SoundScan Lite Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · App: SoundScan Lite (com.soundscan.lite)

In short

SoundScan Lite does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything it records, measures and produces stays on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What the app records and where it is kept

  • Microphone and camera. When you start a recording, the app records sound and video, with a notification showing for as long as it records. The camera is also used to spot light signals you taught the app.
  • Videos you bring in. A video you choose to analyse is copied into the app with a SHA-256 fingerprint of the file.
  • What the app makes from them. Event lists, sound measurements (Hz, note, dB), spectrum pictures, partitions, reports and PDF proofs.

All of this is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone. None of it is uploaded anywhere by the app. It leaves the phone only if you share a file yourself, with Android's share or open buttons, to an app you choose.

Network use

The app has internet permission for one optional feature: the HzCreator remote. When you switch it on, it exchanges play/stop commands and keypad notes with your own computer on your own local network (WiFi or your private VPN). It sends no recordings and contacts no server run by the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Microphone, camera: to record and to detect the signals you set.
  • Notifications: to show that a recording or an analysis is running, and its result.
  • Alarms & reminders, run at startup: to start the daily analysis at the time you set.
  • Internet, network and WiFi state: only for the HzCreator remote described above.

Deleting your data

You can delete events, proofs and recordings inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held elsewhere to delete.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations and filming people differ by country and region. You are responsible for using the app only where and how the law allows. The app's reports describe what was measured. They are not legal advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
